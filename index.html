About Me

Hello! I am Isti Tiandini, a fresh graduate with a Bachelor of Applied Science in Internet Engineering Technology from Universitas Gadjah Mada (GPA 3.55).

I specialize in Web Development, IoT Systems, and UI/UX Design. I have hands-on experience building and managing production-ready websites using HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and WordPress (Elementor & Divi), backed by a solid understanding of web architecture and technical troubleshooting.

Internet of Things

Development of the “DragonLight” end-to-end IoT system for my final project, specifically designed for remote control and energy monitoring of dragon fruit plant lighting. I played a full role in every stage, from planning and designing the IoT hardware and application to conducting hands-on system testing.

Technical development focuses on:

  • Hardware Infrastructure & Networking: Configuring a hardware setup based on the ESP32 microcontroller, integrated with relay components and SCT-013 current sensors to process field data and connect it to the internet.
  • Server, Application, & Cloud Integration: Building the DragonLight mobile app using the MIT App Inventor platform, which seamlessly connects to the Firebase real-time database, Google Spreadsheets, and Google Apps Script for reliable data synchronization and history logging.
  • Integrated Troubleshooting: Comprehensively diagnose and resolve various data communication issues between physical hardware, cloud servers, and the application interface.
  • User-Centric Communication: Designing straightforward and easy-to-understand application instructions and interfaces without complex technical jargon, ensuring this plantation monitoring system can be easily operated by non-technical users.
